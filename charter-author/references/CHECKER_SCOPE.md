# Portable checker scope

The local outcome standard and checker implement the complete published ten-field format and result-reference subset. Numbered-result recognition and wave/task reference scanning derive from the original campaign validator's outcome/result logic. The public checker adds explicit field completeness and proof-reference findings.

The full institutional campaign validator also checks tracker row fidelity, model lanes, a protected constitution, private registers and application-specific paths. Those depend on house infrastructure and are outside this standalone package. A successful check proves structural consistency only. It does not establish source authority, behavior or role independence.
