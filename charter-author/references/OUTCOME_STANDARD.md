# Outcome contract standard

A charter describes the selected outcome, fixed proof and source standing. The ten fields are F1 outcome, F2 proof, F3 results, F4 not-done conditions, F5 grace, F6 ownership boundary, F7 dependencies, F8 work/roles/settings, F9 status and F10 sources.

Preserve each requirement's source and standing. Stated requirements are established by authorized source decisions. Floated proposals and author-drafted details remain attributed. Keep proposed detail available for review and distinct from an already accepted requirement.

Map every selected requirement to a result. Map every required result to a proof step. Fix the proof before implementation, with observable thresholds whose origin is explicit. Record meaningful partial coverage and real gaps.

Every part of work serves a selected result. Changes to requirements follow their actual authority and remain source-linked. A failed implementation does not change the acceptance target. A picture or simulated mechanism proves only its bounded claim.

Implementation choices within the charter's grace and writable scope can be resolved by their assigned owner. External authority and protected settings remain explicit. Keep neighboring ownership understandable without turning it into an unrelated restriction.

The supplied portable structure checker validates fields, result numbers and references. It does not connect to a tracker or enforce model/provider assignment, protected-tree policy, source rights or actual behavioral proof.
