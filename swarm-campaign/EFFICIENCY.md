# Continuation and recovery

Reuse accepted work and unchanged build results. Keep each actor in its task history where supported. At a failure, record the concrete observation, diagnose it and change the approach when it repeats. Fix routine errors within scope.

Escalate only what needs another owner's input, new authority or an indispensable unavailable dependency. State proven, attempted, inferred and unknown. Advance unrelated ready work. Avoid always-on polling and speculative framework changes. At a source change, review the affected dependency and receiving tests before freezing the new result.
